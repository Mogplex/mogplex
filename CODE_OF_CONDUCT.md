# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Mogplex is an engineering project. We want discussion here to be sharp, useful, and respectful.

People participating in this repo are expected to:

- be respectful in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions
- criticize ideas, code, and tradeoffs without attacking people
- assume good intent while still being clear and direct
- keep feedback actionable and relevant to the work
- respect security and privacy boundaries

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, or intimidation
- hate speech or discriminatory language
- doxxing or publishing private information without permission
- repeated bad-faith disruption, trolling, or spam
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this policy. They may also restrict participation from accounts that repeatedly or seriously violate it.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through the repository owner's published contact channel instead of opening a public issue.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to issues, pull requests, code review, and other project spaces controlled by the maintainers.
