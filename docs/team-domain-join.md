# Let teammates join

Open Settings, then Teams. Expand a team to manage access.
Team owners and admins can also open the Members page for the team.

- **Add someone who already signed up:** Enter their verified email, then
  select **Add member**. This adds the account with Developer access.
- **Allow people from this email domain to join:** Turn this on to let people
  with a verified email on the shown domain join as Developers at sign-in.
  The domain comes from the email that the manager verified.
  This control does not support shared email services, such as Gmail.

The control starts off. It matches the exact domain. Subdomains do not match.
People who already have accounts join at their next sign-in.
People who sign up with email join after they verify their address.
Current roles stay the same.

Turn the control off to stop future joins. Current members keep their access.
Remove a member to stop that person from an automatic join.
An owner or admin can still add that account explicitly.

GitHub membership does not give access to a team in Mogplex.
